Our story

A small kitchen with a big fire.

Olive & Ember started as a dream between two friends who grew up cooking with their grandmothers by the sea. Today it's a warm, relaxed place where food is shared and conversations last long after dessert.

We bake our bread every morning, work with local farmers, and change the menu with the seasons.
